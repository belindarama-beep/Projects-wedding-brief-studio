Device framework v2 — three registers, seven devices, one decision table Every device below has a specific job. A real document draws from more than one register across its pages — using only one register end to end, in either direction, is itself a flaw: all-weighted gets heavy and expensive to print, all-gestural goes thin across nine pages.
The decision table
Weighted — mass and shadow, used deliberately
Arch stack The primary device. Cover, and the page carrying the central idea — the one moment of full presence.
Disc trio A second beat of real weight elsewhere in the document — sparingly, never twice.
Edge composition A narrative-heavy page that still needs to carry real weight, geometry pushed to the edge.
Gestural — precision instead of mass, almost no ink
Fine-line arch A quieter page that still needs the brand mark, not just any device.
Ink-sweep circle One more beat of variety on a light page — rhythm from the dots, not a second line.
Gestural sprig A small sign of craft in a margin — a gesture toward florals, never a specific arrangement.
Functional — does content work, not just decoration
Colour rail Any page naming several distinct colour or material decisions at once.
Rule of thumb: a nine-page document should show at least two registers, not one repeated with variations.
